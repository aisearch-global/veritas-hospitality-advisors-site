Step 1 of 2 — Owner Reporting
What your monthly reporting covers

How often you receive formal reports — and whether they show the full financial picture.

0 of 3 answered
How often do you receive a formal written report from your hotel management team?
Does your monthly report show Gross Operating Profit (GOP) — not just revenue? GOP = Revenue minus departmental expenses. It's what the hotel actually earns before fixed costs.
When you receive financial reports, can you trace a revenue figure back to its source? If your report says "Room Revenue: ₹18 lakhs," can you see which bookings, channels, and dates make up that number?

Your oversight of your ownership position

Whether you have the information and structures to protect your asset.

Do you know your hotel's current RevPAR — and how it compares to your competitive set? RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room) = Average Daily Rate × Occupancy. It's the standard measure of room revenue performance.
Have you ever commissioned an independent review of your hotel's financial performance?
How would you describe the depth and consistency of your hotel's financial reporting?
In the last 12 months, have you received a report that raised a concern you couldn't follow up on because the underlying data wasn't available?
Overall — how well does your current oversight of your hotel match the level of oversight you'd expect for an asset of this value?

Step 1 of 2 — Commercial Performance
How your hotel generates revenue

OTA dependency, rate management, and whether you're seeing all the revenue your hotel generates.

0 of 4 answered
Do you know what percentage of your room revenue comes from OTAs versus direct bookings?
Do you know what your hotel paid in OTA commissions last financial year? OTA commissions are typically 15–25% of room revenue. On ₹1 crore in OTA revenue, that's ₹15–25 lakhs leaving your hotel annually.
Has your hotel's Average Daily Rate (ADR) increased in line with your costs over the past two years?
When your hotel offers a discounted rate, do you approve it — or does management decide independently?
