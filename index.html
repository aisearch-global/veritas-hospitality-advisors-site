What this is: A structured self-assessment to help you identify where your hotel's financial reporting, commercial performance, or operational oversight may have gaps. Results are indicative only — not a financial audit or legal opinion. What this is not: A technology audit, a hotel operations tool, or a guest-experience assessment.

Section 01

Owner Reporting

What you receive from your management team — and whether it gives you a complete picture.

How often do you receive a formal written report from your hotel management team?
Does your monthly report show Gross Operating Profit (GOP) — not just revenue? GOP = Revenue minus departmental expenses. It's what the hotel actually earns before fixed costs.
When you receive financial reports, can you trace a revenue figure back to its source? If your report says "Room Revenue: ₹18 lakhs," can you verify which bookings, channels, and dates make up that number?
Section 02

Commercial Performance

How your hotel generates revenue — and whether you're seeing all of it.

Do you know what percentage of your room revenue comes from OTAs versus direct bookings?
Do you know what your hotel paid in OTA commissions last financial year? OTA commissions are typically 15–25% of room revenue. On ₹1 crore in OTA revenue, that's ₹15–25 lakhs leaving your hotel annually.
Has your hotel's Average Daily Rate (ADR) increased in line with your costs over the past two years?
When your hotel offers a discounted rate, do you approve it — or does management decide independently?
Section 03

Operational Governance

How decisions are made in your hotel — and whether you have oversight of them.

Is there a written operating plan or annual budget that your management team works against?
If your General Manager resigned tomorrow, how confident are you in the continuity of your hotel's operations?
Are capital expenditure decisions (above a set threshold) formally approved by you before they're committed?
Section 04

Owner Position

Whether you have the information and oversight to protect your ownership position.

Do you know your hotel's current RevPAR — and how it compares to your competitive set? RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room) is the standard measure of room revenue performance. It equals Average Daily Rate × Occupancy.
Have you ever commissioned an independent review of your hotel's financial performance — separate from your management team's reporting?
How would you describe your level of confidence in the accuracy of your hotel's financial reporting?
In the last 12 months, have you received a report that raised a concern you couldn't follow up on because the underlying data wasn't available?
Overall — how well does your current oversight of your hotel match the level of oversight you'd expect for an asset of this value?

Results are calculated locally. No data is sent anywhere.

Does Veritas operate hotels?

No. Veritas does not operate hotels, manage hotel staff, or replace your management team. Veritas works as an independent advisor on behalf of the owner — reviewing commercial performance, identifying gaps, and providing structured oversight. The operator stays in place. The owner gets a second set of expert eyes.

Who does Veritas work for?

Veritas works exclusively for independent hotel and resort owners in India. We have no relationship with hotel management companies, hotel brands, or operators. Every recommendation Veritas makes is in the owner's interest — not the brand's, not the operator's, not the management's.

What is hotel owner advisory?

Independent oversight of your hotel's commercial performance — revenue, occupancy, cost structure, staffing, and reporting — conducted on your behalf. Large hotel groups have this built into their corporate structure. Independent owners rarely do. Veritas fills that gap, without the overhead of a brand flag or a management contract.
