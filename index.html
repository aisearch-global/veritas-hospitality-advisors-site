Common questions

What owners ask us

Does Veritas operate hotels?

No. Veritas does not operate hotels, manage hotel staff, or replace your management team. Veritas works as an independent advisor on behalf of the owner — reviewing commercial performance, identifying gaps, and providing structured oversight. The operator stays in place. The owner gets a second set of expert eyes.

Who does Veritas work for?

Veritas works exclusively for independent hotel and resort owners in India. We have no relationship with hotel management companies, hotel brands, or operators. Every recommendation Veritas makes is in the owner's interest — not the brand's, not the operator's, not the management's.

What is hotel owner advisory?

Independent oversight of your hotel's commercial performance — revenue, occupancy, cost structure, staffing, and reporting — conducted on your behalf. Large hotel groups have this built into their corporate structure. Independent owners rarely do. Veritas fills that gap, without the overhead of a brand flag or a management contract.

What has Vaishakh actually run?

Properties under Accor, Wyndham, ITC, and independent flags across India. At Accor Mercure Chennai, he achieved a 100% brand audit score and the highest employee engagement score among the brand's properties. At Lyfe Hotels Bhubaneswar, he delivered ₹32 crore revenue at 85% occupancy and was named Best GM in Odisha. He has also managed Indian-owned hotel assets in the USA.

How is Veritas paid?

Veritas is paid by the owner only: a fixed monthly retainer scaled to the property's business volume, plus a performance-linked fee agreed in the engagement letter. Veritas takes no commissions or referral fees from OTAs, vendors, brands or suppliers.

How does an engagement typically begin?

Every engagement begins with a confidential conversation — no obligation and no pitch. Vaishakh personally handles every initial discussion. If structured advisory is the right fit, scope and terms are agreed from there. There is no junior team, no handoff, no process before the conversation.
