02 — Service

Operational Governance
reducing leakage, lifting GOP

Standards deployment, cost discipline, manpower analysis, procurement review, and service quality governance — conducted on the owner's behalf, working alongside the management team's own reporting.

What Veritas reviews

Where GOP leaks — and how to stop it

01

LSOP Audit & Deployment

Are your standard operating procedures actually in use, or are they a filing exercise? Veritas audits your LSOP compliance across departments — front office, food & beverage, housekeeping, maintenance — and identifies where gaps in standards are creating service inconsistency, waste, and guest dissatisfaction.

02

Productivity & Manpower Analysis

Payroll is typically the single largest controllable cost in a hotel. Veritas reviews your staffing ratios, shift patterns, and productivity metrics against your actual revenue levels — identifying over-staffing in slow periods, under-staffing that creates service failures, and outsourcing arrangements that warrant renegotiation.

03

Cost Control Frameworks

Food cost, beverage cost, utility consumption, maintenance spend — each category has both a market norm and your property's specific history. Veritas establishes where you sit against both, identifies the variance drivers, and recommends control frameworks that the property team can actually operate without creating new bureaucracy.

04

Vendor & Procurement Review

Long-standing vendor relationships often survive on inertia rather than value. Veritas reviews your key procurement contracts, pricing against current market, and whether suppliers are performing to agreement. This includes OTA commission structures, linen and laundry contracts, food and beverage suppliers, and technology subscriptions.

05

Service Quality Governance

Guest satisfaction scores are a lagging indicator — they tell you something went wrong, not why or where. Veritas establishes a leading-indicator framework: operational checkpoints that predict guest experience outcomes before they show up in reviews, and an escalation structure that reaches the owner before the problem becomes a pattern.

"Operating decisions made against an owner-approved plan — with clear authority, clear escalation, and independent review."

The Veritas mandate

Why operational governance requires an outside view

Hotel teams run on day-to-day relationships — with suppliers, heads of department and staff. That is how good hotels operate. It also means that chronic inefficiencies, underperforming vendors, and standards drift are rarely raised internally until they become crises. Veritas reviews operations from the outside, with no existing relationships to protect and a single mandate: the owner's return. That independence is what makes the review useful.

What this delivers for owners

Reduced leakage. Stronger GOP.

01 A clear view of where payroll cost is misaligned with revenue — and a staffing model that reflects actual demand
02 Standards compliance that is audited, not assumed — across every department that affects guest experience
03 Cost benchmarks for all major controllable categories, with variance explained and action plans in place
04 Vendor contracts reviewed against market — with renegotiation recommendations that have numbers behind them
05 Service quality governance that is predictive, not reactive — catching problems before they reach the guest
06 An operating plan that the owner has approved — with clear authority levels and an escalation path that reaches you

How Veritas engages

Structured review, then active oversight

Operational governance work begins with a structured audit of current practice — not a checklist exercise, but a property-level review that identifies the specific gaps between what the owner expects and what is actually happening.

The audit produces a prioritised roadmap: the items with the highest cost-to-fix and highest benefit. Veritas then moves into active oversight, conducting monthly operational reviews and maintaining the governance framework on the owner's behalf.

Importantly, Veritas works alongside your GM and department heads — not against them. The aim is to give the property team a clearer framework to operate within, and the owner a clearer picture of how that framework is performing.

Stage 1 · Days 0–30

Operational Audit & Roadmap

LSOP compliance review. Staffing and productivity analysis. Cost category benchmarking. Vendor contract assessment. Service quality checkpoint framework. Delivered as a structured audit with prioritised findings and a 90-day improvement roadmap.

Stage 2 · Day 30 onwards

Active Operational Governance

Monthly operational review against the approved plan. Cost variance monitoring. Standards compliance tracking. Vendor performance review. Service quality escalation management. Owner-facing reporting on GOP performance and operational discipline.
